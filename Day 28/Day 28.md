# Day 28/60 – Claude AI Challenge
Hospital Admissions & Prior Authorization Simulator

# Overview:

On Day 28 of the Claude AI Challenge, I explored hospital admission workflows and Prior Authorization (PA) decision-making using an interactive simulator. This project demonstrates how AI-assisted analysis can support operational teams in making faster, more compliant admission decisions.

# The repository includes:

* A visual admission readiness dashboard (image)
* An HTML-based simulator modeling real-world hospital admission and PA logic

# Objective:

To understand how operational, clinical, and payer-related factors interact during hospital admissions—and how AI can help reduce delays, denials, and manual rework.

# Key Learnings:

* Hospital admission decisions rely on both clinical justification and payer policy alignment
* Prior Authorization is often delayed due to incomplete documentation rather than medical necessity
* Simulating admission workflows helps identify gaps before a case reaches utilization review
* AI-driven readiness scoring can standardize decisions across teams

# Operational Insight:


Early Prior Authorization checks at the admission stage significantly reduce downstream denials and length of stay.
Embedding PA validation into admission workflows improves operational efficiency and enhances patient experience.

# Why This Matters

Healthcare operations are complex and time-sensitive. Tools like this simulator show how AI can bring clarity, consistency, and speed to critical admission decisions.
