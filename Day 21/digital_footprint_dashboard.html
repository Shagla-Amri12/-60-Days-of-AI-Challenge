🎯 Final Verdict

Digital Footprint: Significant (🟠 74/100, Estimate). Fifteen services across 8 distinct data domains, concentrated mainly under Google (27% of services, Fact) and Meta, give a wide and fairly joined-up view of daily activity — search, video, messaging, payments and photos all sit with two companies.

Privacy: Fair (🟠 42/100, Estimate). The mix leans toward high-telemetry, ad-funded apps (social video, social camera apps, free-to-play games) rather than privacy-first tools, which is typical and not in itself a problem — but it does mean exposure is on the higher side by default.

  • Highest-leverage fix: reduce ad personalization on Google and Meta accounts — touches the largest share of services at once.
  • Second priority: tighten location permissions on social and gaming apps to "while using."
  • Longer-term: spreading services across more independent providers would lower ecosystem concentration below 20%.
How to read this report: All facts (service count, parent companies, ecosystem concentration) are calculated directly from the 15 services you listed. Everything else — scores, heatmap intensities, the risk radar, the digital twin profile, the data collection matrix, and the value ranking — is a modeled estimate based on typical, publicly known behavior of these categories of apps. This dashboard does not access your accounts, any data broker, or any private or leaked database, and nothing here should be treated as a certain or verified fact about you.
