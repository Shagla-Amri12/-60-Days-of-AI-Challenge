# Day 23/60 — Claude AI Challenge  
# AI Study Assistant | Customer & MVP Blueprint (India)

This repository contains the **Customer & MVP Blueprint** and a **futuristic AI dashboard concept** built as part of my **60 Day Claude AI Challenge**.

# Biggest Customer Insight:
Students don’t struggle with lack of content — they struggle with **information overload, fragmented tools, and poor retention**.  
Using multiple apps (YouTube, PDFs, notes, planners) creates confusion, not productivity.  
What students actually want is **clarity on what to study, when to study, and how well they’re retaining it**.

# Key MVP Feature Discovered:
**One-click study loop:**  
**Lecture / PDF → Smart Summary → Auto Quiz → Personalized Study Plan**

This single flow solves three core problems at once:
- Reduces information overload  
- Improves retention through active recall  
- Removes “what should I study next?” confusion  

When progress is visualized (retention %, streaks, weak topics), motivation turns into a daily habit.

# Key Learnings:
- Progress visibility is more powerful than raw AI features  
- Retention metrics matter more than content volume  
- Indian students need **simple, fast, affordable** AI tools — not complex platforms  
- The MVP should focus on **one strong daily habit**, not many features  

# Outcome:
A validated MVP direction focused on:
- AI Summaries  
- Auto Quiz Generation  
- Personalized Study Planning  
- Dashboard-driven motivation & habit formation
