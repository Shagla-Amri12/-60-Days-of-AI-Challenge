Part 1 — The Roast

Headline 2/10

Recruiter's 3-second read: "Another student. Next."

❌ The problem: "Student at Integral University" tells a recruiter your enrollment status and nothing about what you can actually do. It's the LinkedIn equivalent of a business card with only your school's name on it.
🧠 Why it hurts: Headlines are the #1 thing LinkedIn search indexes on. Recruiters searching "Python developer intern" or "React JS intern" will never surface your profile, because none of those keywords exist in your headline.
🔍 Invisible cost: You're invisible in recruiter search. Every recruiter using LinkedIn Recruiter's keyword filters — which is most of them — skips you before they even open your profile.

About — First 2 Lines 0/10

There's nothing to react to — because there's nothing there.

❌ The problem: No About section at all.
🧠 Why it hurts: The About section's first two lines are the most valuable real estate on your entire profile — it's what shows before "see more." An empty About section signals "I haven't taken this seriously," even if that's not true.
🔍 Invisible cost: Recruiters skimming search results often decide whether to click into a profile based on the About preview alone. Yours currently has zero pull.

About — Full Section 0/10

Same story — a blank page where your pitch should be.

❌ The problem: Missing entirely.
🧠 Why it hurts: This is where you're supposed to answer "what do you do, who do you help, why should I care" in your own voice. Without it, a recruiter has to guess your story from a bare skills list — and busy people don't guess, they move on.

Experience 0/10

"No experience listed — is this even a real, active profile?"

❌ The problem: No experience section at all — not even projects, coursework, or a personal site listed as an entry.
🧠 Why it hurts: As a student without a job history yet, this section is exactly where you're supposed to list personal projects, coursework, hackathons, or freelance work instead. Leaving it blank doesn't read as "I'm a student," it reads as "I haven't built anything."
🔍 Invisible cost: You already have a roadmap listing 5 concrete projects (Portfolio, Full-Stack Notes App, GitHub Repo Finder, Weather Dashboard, Blog/CMS). Once even one of these is built, it belongs here — right now that opportunity is sitting completely unused.

Skills & Keywords 4/10

"Okay, they know some tools — but for which role?"

❌ The problem: Python, React.js, JavaScript, HTML, CSS — a decent starter list, but generic and not aligned to your actual target (Python Dev + AI/ML). There's no signal of frameworks, ML libraries, or backend tools recruiters filter for.
🧠 Why it hurts: Skills are the second-biggest LinkedIn search signal after your headline. Five broad web-dev-flavored skills won't surface you for AI/ML-specific searches like "pandas," "scikit-learn," or "Django REST."
🔍 Invisible cost: You're partially findable for frontend roles, essentially invisible for AI/ML ones — despite that being half your stated goal.

Overall Profile Strength

12/100

A near-empty shell with a generic headline. Good news: because there's almost nothing here, every change you make is pure upside — there's no bad copy to undo, just blank space to fill correctly.
