# AI-Powered LinkedIn Optimizer

Today I built an AI-Powered LinkedIn Optimizer that analyzes LinkedIn profiles like an experienced recruiter and provides actionable recommendations to improve personal branding, profile visibility, and career opportunities.

Instead of simply pointing out weaknesses, the AI delivers a complete optimization strategy—including profile audits, recruiter-focused suggestions, keyword optimization, profile score improvements, and a personalized roadmap for growth.

# Features
* AI-Powered LinkedIn Profile Audit
* Honest Profile Roast & Feedback
* Recruiter-Friendly Headline Suggestions
* AI-Generated Professional About Section
* Before vs After Profile Score Comparison
*  Skills & Recruiter Keyword Optimization
* Experience Section Improvement Suggestions
* Personalized 7-Day LinkedIn Growth Plan
* Personal Branding Recommendations
* Modern Terminal-Inspired Responsive UI
* What impressed me the most while building it?

The feature that impressed me the most was the AI Profile Rebuild Engine.

Instead of giving generic advice, it analyzes every section of the profile individually, explains why it's hurting recruiter visibility, and then generates recruiter-optimized improvements with clear reasoning. Seeing the profile transform from a 12/100 profile into a recruiter-ready 72/100 profile made the entire experience feel like having a real career coach powered by AI.
