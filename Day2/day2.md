 Day 2 of the ABTalks 60 Days Claude Challenge

Today's focus was Prompt Engineering—the skill of writing clear and effective instructions to get better results from AI tools like Claude.

In this project, I explored:
• What Prompt Engineering is
• Why it matters when working with AI
• Weak vs. engineered prompts
• Practical benefits of better prompting
• A LinkedIn-ready visual explaining the concept

Key takeaway:
"Better prompts lead to better outputs."

This repository documents my learning journey as I build AI skills, improve my prompting techniques, and explore real-world applications of AI through the 60 Days Claude Challenge.
#FOR EXAMPLE
. Weak Prompt

Build an AI chatbot.

Output:
Basic chatbot explanation.

. Strong Prompt

Act as a Senior AI Engineer. Design an AI customer support chatbot using Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, and RAG. Include architecture and deployment.

Output:
Complete system architecture, tech stack, database design, and deployment plan.
