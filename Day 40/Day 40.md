# Project: The Green Room – Mock Interview Coach

# Objective

Build a custom AI-powered interview assistant that helps job seekers practice realistic mock interviews, receive personalized feedback, and improve their confidence before facing real interviews.

# Overview

**The Green Room – Mock Interview Coach** is an AI assistant that simulates a real interview experience by asking role-specific questions based on the user's target job and experience level. After the interview, it generates a comprehensive evaluation report highlighting strengths, areas for improvement, question-by-question feedback, and an overall interview readiness score.

Instead of simply answering questions, the assistant acts like an experienced interviewer, creating a structured and interactive interview environment that helps users identify gaps in their preparation.

# Share your custom AI Assistant and explain what problem it solves

Preparing for technical and behavioral interviews without expert guidance can be overwhelming. Most candidates don't know whether their answers are strong enough or what skills they need to improve.

**The Green Room** solves this problem by providing an AI-powered mock interview experience that:

* Conducts realistic, role-specific interviews
* Evaluates responses intelligently
* Provides actionable feedback instead of generic suggestions
* Highlights strengths and improvement areas
* Tracks interview readiness with a detailed performance score

This allows candidates to practice anytime, improve continuously, and approach real interviews with greater confidence.

Watching the assistant maintain context throughout the interview and finally transform the complete conversation into a structured evaluation report demonstrated how powerful prompt engineering can be when combined with thoughtful UI design.
