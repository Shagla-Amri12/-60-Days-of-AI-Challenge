About NutriScope

NutriScope is an open, browser-based nutrition tracker built for educational use. All computations run locally in your browser — no data is sent to any server. Nutritional values are approximate and may vary based on preparation method, food variety, and source. The tool is designed to raise awareness about dietary patterns, not to replace professional nutritional counselling. BMI categories used follow WHO classifications. Risk analysis flags are based on general population evidence and may not apply to every individual.
