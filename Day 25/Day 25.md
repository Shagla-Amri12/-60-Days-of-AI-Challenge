# Day 25 / 60 — Claude AI Startup Challenge
# AI Shark Tank Pitch Simulator
# Overview:

This project documents my Day 25 progress in the 60-Day Claude AI Challenge, where I used an AI-powered Shark Tank–style pitch simulator to pressure-test a startup idea from an investor’s perspective.

The startup, EcoPack, was evaluated by AI investors across market size, innovation, business model, execution, and investment readiness. The goal was not to get a perfect score, but to identify real weaknesses, tough questions, and decision-making gaps that founders face in actual fundraising conversations.

# Objectives:
Simulate a real investor pitch environment using AI
Test the clarity and scalability of a sustainability-focused startup idea
Understand how investors evaluate TAM, defensibility, and unit economics
Practice answering high-pressure, critical investor questions
Learn how to refine ideas based on structured feedback, not intuition
# Startup Idea — EcoPack:

EcoPack is a B2B subscription platform that supplies small and mid-size DTC e-commerce brands with compostable packaging made from agricultural waste, priced at parity with plastic. The mission is to reduce packaging waste without increasing costs for sellers.

# Investment Decision (AI Investors):

Overall Score: 58 / 100
Strong signals in market potential and execution intent
Gaps identified in competitive moat, pricing defensibility, and margins
Verdict: Mixed interest — promising idea, needs stronger fundamentals

# Most Challenging Question Asked by AI Investors:

“What stops a well-funded competitor from copying this in six months?”

This question highlighted the importance of:

Supply-chain defensibility
Cost advantages at scale
Customer lock-in beyond sustainability claims

# Key Learnings:
A good idea is not enough — moats and economics matter most
Investors prioritize execution risk over vision
Hard questions reveal blind spots faster than validation
AI simulations are valuable tools for founder-level thinking

# Why This Project Matters:

This exercise helped shift my mindset from “pitching an idea” to “defending a business.”
It’s a practical step toward building investor-ready clarity before real-world fundraising.
