# Day 50/60 — Defend Your Experience

> **AI-Powered Interview Preparation & Resume Defense Platform**

# Objective

The objective of **Defend Your Experience** is to help candidates go beyond building a strong resume by preparing them to confidently explain, justify, and defend every project, achievement, and skill during real interviews through AI-powered questioning and personalized feedback.

# Overview

**Defend Your Experience** is an AI-powered interview preparation platform designed to simulate real interview experiences. Rather than simply reviewing resumes, the application extracts key claims from a candidate's resume, challenges them with intelligent follow-up questions, evaluates the quality of their responses, and generates a detailed defense report.

The platform enables users to identify weak areas, improve communication, strengthen technical explanations, and build confidence before facing actual interviews.
