# Day 30/60 — Claude AI Challenge
AI Supply Chain Builder Simulation

# Objective:

The objective of this project was to design and optimize an end-to-end supply chain for a high-growth cosmetics business using an AI-powered simulation.
The focus was on making realistic operational decisions while balancing:

Cost efficiency
Delivery speed
Risk management
Customer satisfaction
Sustainability

# Project Overview:

This simulation walks through the complete supply chain decision lifecycle—from supplier selection to inventory strategy—showing how each choice impacts overall business performance.

Using AI-generated scenarios, I built a supply chain for Marlow Goods, a cosmetics company serving Germany and France, and evaluated outcomes through live business metrics.

# Final Results:
Final Supply Chain Score: 67 / 100
Demand Type: High Growth
Product Category: Cosmetics

# Key Optimization Decision

Highest Impact Decision:
* Multiple Suppliers + Distributed Warehouses

This combination significantly improved risk resilience and customer satisfaction, ensuring continuity during disruptions and faster regional fulfillment—while increasing operational complexity.

# Key Learnings:
* Supply chain optimization is about trade-offs, not perfect scores
* Resilience and diversification matter more than lowest cost in growth scenarios
* Early decisions create cascading downstream effects
* AI simulations enable safe experimentation with real-world business logic

# Decision Summary:
* Supplier Strategy: Multiple Suppliers
* Factory Location: Nearshore Factory
* Warehouse Strategy: Distributed Warehouses
* Transportation Method: Rail
* Inventory Strategy: High Inventory

# Why This Project Matters:

This project strengthened my understanding of AI-assisted decision-making, operational strategy, and how complex systems behave under different constraints—bridging the gap between theory and real-world execution.
