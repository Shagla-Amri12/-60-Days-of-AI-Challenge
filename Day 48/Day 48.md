# Day 48/60 — The Verdict Engine
# First-Time EV Buyer: Compare & Decide

An AI-powered decision intelligence dashboard that helps first-time electric vehicle buyers compare multiple EVs using weighted scoring, transparent research, and verified data sources. Instead of relying on marketing claims, users can prioritize what matters most and receive a live recommendation based on their preferences.

# Objective

To build an interactive AI-assisted decision support tool that simplifies EV purchasing by combining research, comparison, and customizable weighted analysis into a single intuitive dashboard.

# Overview

The Verdict Engine enables users to compare popular electric vehicles across five critical buying factors:

* Purchase Price
* EPA Driving Range
* DC Fast Charging Speed
* 5-Year Cost of Ownership
* Safety Ratings

Users can adjust the importance of each criterion using interactive sliders, and the dashboard instantly recalculates rankings based on their priorities. The project also includes complete research transparency through a detailed methodology section and source citations.

# Features
Interactive weighted scoring system
Live ranking updates
Side-by-side EV comparison
Responsive dark-themed dashboard
Research methodology panel
Verified source references
Data confidence indicators (Sourced / Estimate / Data Gap)
User-friendly decision support interface

# Biggest Insight

The biggest lesson from this project was that there is no universally "best" EV. The optimal choice changes depending on what the buyer values most.

# Most Surprising AI Finding

While many people expect the Tesla Model 3 to dominate every comparison, the AI revealed that the Hyundai Ioniq 5 became the top recommendation under balanced priorities because of its strong mix of affordability, charging speed, and safety. It also highlighted how missing real-world ownership or safety data can significantly reduce confidence in recommendations.
