# Day 16 — Stock Fundamental Research Skill | 60 Days of Claude AI Challenge

# Overview:

On Day 16, I designed and tested a custom AI skill focused on **Stock Fundamental Research**. The objective was to transform stock analysis into a reusable AI workflow capable of generating structured, evidence-based research reports.

# Objective:

Build a custom skill that can:

* Analyze company fundamentals
* Review valuation metrics (P/E, ROE, ROCE, EV/EBITDA)
* Generate stock price visualizations
* Track ownership and shareholding trends
* Identify growth opportunities and risk factors
* Produce investor-friendly research reports

# Test Case:

**Analyzed Stock:** Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)

# What Surprised Me Most:

The generated research went beyond displaying stock data and combined **valuation, financial quality, ownership trends, business strengths, and risk indicators into one structured dashboard**.

One interesting observation was seeing multiple signals together:

* Historically lower valuation levels
* Strong return ratios (ROE)
* Debt-free position
* Clear risk watch-points
* Financial snapshot and ownership trends in one report

This made the output feel closer to a research workflow than a standard AI-generated response.

# Key Learnings:

* AI skills become more powerful when converted into reusable systems.
* Structured prompts improve consistency and report quality.
* Financial insights become easier to interpret when combined with visual reporting.

# Workflow

Create Skill → Configure Instructions → Generate Report → Review Metrics → Validate Output → Document Findings
