Monthly Summary —

Quick tips for students

Pay yourself first.
Move a small fixed amount to savings the day your allowance lands, before you spend on anything else.
Use the 50/30/20 idea loosely.
Roughly 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings — adjust the split to fit a student budget.
Audit subscriptions monthly.
Small recurring charges are the easiest overspending to miss.
Track before you cut.
You can't fix what you haven't measured — log every expense for at least 2 weeks.

Useful resources
