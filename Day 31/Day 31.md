# Day 31 – AI Supply Chain Control Tower | 60 Days Claude AI Challenge

# Overview:
On Day 31 of the #60DaysClaudeChallenge by ABTalks, I completed an interactive AI Supply Chain Control Tower simulation where I took on the role of Head of Operations. The objective was to monitor a live global supply chain, respond to operational disruptions in real time, and make strategic decisions that balanced customer satisfaction, operational efficiency, and financial performance.

This project demonstrates how AI-powered decision support systems help organizations proactively manage supply chain risks, optimize logistics, and maintain business continuity under pressure.

# Objective:

The primary objective of this simulation was to:

Monitor live supply chain KPIs.
Respond quickly to operational disruptions.
Prioritize alerts based on business impact.
Maintain high customer satisfaction and service levels.
Protect revenue while minimizing operational costs.
Experience how AI enhances operational decision-making through real-time analytics and intelligent recommendations.

# Key Learnings:
 1. AI Enables Faster Operational Decisions

Real-time AI insights significantly reduce decision-making time during operational disruptions.

 2. KPIs Tell the Complete Story

Monitoring multiple KPIs together provides a holistic view of operational performance instead of relying on a single metric.

 3. Prioritization is Critical

Not every alert requires immediate action. Identifying the highest-impact issues first is essential for maintaining service levels.

 4. Supply Chains are Highly Connected

A delay in one area can quickly affect inventory, transportation, customer satisfaction, and revenue, emphasizing the need for end-to-end visibility.

 5. Data-Driven Decisions Reduce Risk

Using AI-generated insights helps minimize guesswork and supports more informed operational choices.

 6. Balancing Cost and Service is Challenging

Improving customer satisfaction while controlling operational costs requires continuous optimization and trade-off analysis.

 7. AI Supports, Humans Decide

While AI provides recommendations and predictive insights, effective outcomes still depend on human judgment and strategic thinking.

 8. Crisis Management Requires Agility

Quick adaptation, prioritization, and continuous monitoring are key to maintaining resilient operations during unexpected events.
