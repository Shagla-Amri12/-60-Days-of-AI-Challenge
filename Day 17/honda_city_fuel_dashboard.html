⚖️ E85 Paradox Analysis

E85 offers an 18% pump price saving (₹82 vs ₹100/L) but delivers a +3.6% running cost penalty due to lower mileage (12.9 km/L vs 16.3 km/L for Petrol E20), meaning you actually pay slightly more per kilometre with E85.


The break-even E85 price is ₹79.1/L — E85 must drop below this for real savings. At current pricing, E85 is close but not yet at parity.


E85's biggest win is CO₂ reduction (0.070 kg/km vs 0.171 for Petrol) — nearly 59% lower emissions, making it the clear choice for eco-conscious drivers willing to trade minor cost efficiency.
